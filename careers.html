Join the deal desk

We're a small, remote team that helps people pay less for software without getting tricked. If you love finding the real deal behind the marketing, we'd like to hear from you.

Freelance · Remote

Deal researcher

Verify programmes and offers against official pages, track price history and keep listings current. Detail-obsessed and sceptical of "was" prices.

Freelance · Remote

Contributing writer

Write clear, accurate guides on subscriptions, perks and software buying. Experience in tech or personal-finance writing preferred.

Freelance · Remote

Video creator

Produce short, honest walkthroughs of tools, perks and how-tos for our video library and YouTube.

Part-time · Remote

Community moderator

Review deal tips and contest entries, and help readers with questions.

Commission · Remote

Partnerships lead

Work with app makers on launch deals, sponsorships and contest prizes, always within our editorial rules.

Contract · Remote

Front-end developer

Build fast, accessible calculators and features in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

How we work

  • Fully remote and asynchronous, with flexible hours.
  • Paid per project or per hour, agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Accuracy first: every claim is checked against a primary source.
  • Editorial independence: sponsors never review or approve our content.
