Software & app sale calendar

When software, apps and games are usually cheapest. Fixed dates are exact; "typical windows" are based on when the event has run in recent years, so confirm with the retailer as it approaches.

The biggest software sale day of the year: SaaS annual plans, VPNs, utilities, creative suites and lifetime deals.

Cyber Monday

Many Black Friday software offers extend through Cyber Monday; some vendors add new ones.

Giving Tuesday

A global day of giving. Nonprofit technology programmes and donation tools often promote offers.

New Year productivity sales

Presidents Day (US)

A long weekend of retail sales in the US; some software and hardware sellers join in.
