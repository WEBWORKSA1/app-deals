How to run a good audit

  1. Collect three months of statements. Some subscriptions bill quarterly or yearly, so one month will miss them.
  2. Search for the usual billers: app stores, streaming services, software companies, and anything labelled "subscription", "membership" or "renewal".
  3. Be honest about use. "Rarely" means you could not name the last time you opened it.
  4. Act on the rarely used ones first. Cancel, downgrade, or switch to a family plan or a free alternative.
