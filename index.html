GS

GitHub Student Developer Pack

by GitHub
FreeStudents

Free GitHub Pro, Copilot access and dozens of partner offers while you are a verified student

The single most valuable bundle for anyone learning to code. One verification unlocks GitHub Pro, AI coding help and partner credits for hosting, domains, design and learning platforms.

Eligibility and how to claim

Who qualifies: Students aged 13+ enrolled in a degree or diploma-granting course, verified through GitHub Education.

  1. Sign in to GitHub and open GitHub Education
  2. Apply with your school email or proof of enrolment
  3. Once approved, claim partner offers one by one from the pack page

JE

JetBrains Educational Licenses

by JetBrains
FreeStudentsEducators

Free access to JetBrains professional IDEs for students and teachers

Professional IDEs such as IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm and WebStorm at no cost for coursework and personal learning projects.

Eligibility and how to claim

Who qualifies: Students and teachers at accredited institutions; renew each year while you remain eligible.

  1. Open the JetBrains education page
  2. Apply with a university email, ISIC card or official document
  3. Activate the licence in any JetBrains IDE
Listed October 2026 Official page
AA

AWS Activate

by Amazon Web Services
CreditsStartups

AWS credits, technical support and training for eligible startups

The default cloud credit programme. Self-funded founders can apply directly; larger tiers come through accelerators and investors.

Eligibility and how to claim

Who qualifies: Early-stage startups that meet the programme rules (for example funding stage and company age).

  1. Create or use an AWS account
  2. Apply to AWS Activate with company details
  3. Ask your accelerator or investor for a provider ID for higher tiers
Listed October 2026 Official page
GF

Google for Nonprofits

by Google
FreeNonprofits

Free Google Workspace for Nonprofits, Ad Grants and YouTube Nonprofit Program

Email, documents and free search advertising in one programme. Ad Grants alone can drive meaningful traffic.

Eligibility and how to claim

Who qualifies: Eligible registered nonprofits; excluded entity types are listed by Google.

  1. Check eligibility
  2. Verify your organisation
  3. Activate each product separately
Listed October 2026 Official page
AF

Apple Family Sharing

by Apple
FreeEveryone

Share eligible App Store purchases and subscriptions with up to five family members

Stop buying the same paid app twice in one household.

Eligibility and how to claim

Who qualifies: Apple account holders who set up a family group.

  1. Open Settings and your name, then Family
  2. Invite family members
  3. Turn on purchase sharing

Libby

by OverDrive
FreeEveryone

Free ebooks, audiobooks and magazines with a public library card

Many people pay for audiobooks they could borrow free from their local library.

Eligibility and how to claim

Who qualifies: Anyone with a card from a participating library.

  1. Install Libby
  2. Add your library card
  3. Place holds on popular titles

Questions, answered

Does App.Deals sell software?

No. App.Deals is an independent guide. We research programmes and offers, explain who qualifies and link to the official page. You buy or apply directly with the provider.

How do you make money?

Through advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsored placements from software makers, and reader support. Sponsors never decide our editorial picks. Read our editorial policy.

Are the listings verified?

Each listing is researched from the provider's official page and dated. Terms change, so always confirm on the official page. If something is out of date, use the report form on the deals page.

Is App.Deals connected to any "AppDeals" app or company?

No. App.Deals is a descriptive name for an independent website and is not affiliated with any business, app or trademark using a similar name. See our trademark notice.

Do the tools store my data?

No. Calculators run entirely in your browser. Saved deals and watchlists are stored only on your device. Forms you choose to submit are sent to us by email.
