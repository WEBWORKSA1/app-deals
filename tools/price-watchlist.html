Send your watchlist to our deal desk. When we spot one of your items at or below your target in a public sale, we'll email you.

How to set a good target price

Look at the vendor's own pricing page and note the normal price. For games, price-history sites show previous lows; a target near the last seasonal low is realistic. For software, a target 20–40% below normal is typical during big sales such as Black Friday. Check the sale calendar for when those sales usually happen.
