Why calculators beat percentages

A percentage only tells you how a price compares with another price the seller chose. The questions that decide whether you save money are different: how long you will really use the product, what it costs per year after the introductory period, and whether a free option already does the job. Each tool here answers one of those questions with your own numbers.

Use the deal checker for any offer, the lifetime deal calculator before any pay-once purchase, and run the subscription audit every quarter. Together they take less time than one cancelled subscription saves.
