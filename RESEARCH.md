# Competitive review: 36 sites in the app, software and perks deals niche (October 2026)

## Sites reviewed
**App and software deals:** AppAdvice (Apps Gone Free), AppRaven (formerly AppSliced), 148Apps, BitsDuJour, SharewareOnSale, Giveaway of the Day, MacUpdate, DealFuel, StackSocial, AppSumo, DealMirror, SaaSMantra, PitchGround
**General deal communities:** Slickdeals, DealNews, 9to5Toys (in place of Wirecutter Deals, which blocked fetching), RetailMeNot (blocked), Honey
**Price tracking and games:** camelcamelcamel / Keepa, IsThereAnyDeal, gg.deals, Humble Bundle (blocked), Fanatical, Setapp
**Discovery and alternatives:** Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, OpenSourceAlternative.to, SaaSworthy, G2 Deals, Capterra
**Perks programmes:** GitHub Student Developer Pack, Secret (joinsecret.com), Student Beans, UNiDAYS, TechSoup and Google for Nonprofits, AWS Activate

## Patterns adopted on App.Deals
1. **Original editorial on every listing** (why it's worth it, who qualifies, steps, caveats). AppAdvice and GitHub's pack do this; thin aggregators don't. This matters most for AdSense approval.
2. **Trust and disclosure pages**: about, editorial policy, sponsored labels, privacy, terms, disclaimer, trademark. DealNews and Slickdeals disclose; 9to5Toys and SharewareOnSale were weak here.
3. **One standard card**: initials mark (no third-party logos), type and audience badges, offer line, "listed" date, save, share, official link.
4. **Faceted filters**: audience chips, type, topic, text search, sort, result count, empty state and URL-synced state (Slickdeals, G2, Fanatical).
5. **Free and open-source alternatives** structured as "instead of X" (AlternativeTo, OpenSourceAlternative.to), with licence badges and FAQ schema.
6. **Perks grouped by audience** with eligibility and verification steps (GitHub pack, AWS Activate tiers, Google for Nonprofits steps).
7. **Sale calendar with countdown** (Fanatical and DealMirror countdowns, Giveaway of the Day archive calendar).
8. **Segmented deal alerts** (Slickdeals alerts, BitsDuJour "Daily Bits", AppSumo email capture) as a 3-step form.
9. **"Submit / promote your software" funnel** (BitsDuJour, SharewareOnSale, DealMirror, Secret "List your SaaS") as a 4-step qualifying form with paid add-ons.
10. **Watchlist and target price** (camelcamelcamel, IsThereAnyDeal waitlist), browser-stored with an optional email request.
11. **Calculators** (none of the competitors do this well; this is App.Deals' differentiator for links and dwell time).
12. **Consistent numbers only.** Secret and SharewareOnSale lose trust with mismatched counts, so App.Deals generates every count from the data file at build time.

## Patterns deliberately avoided
- Fake urgency (resetting timers, "only N left" on digital goods).
- Gated content behind sign-up (G2, UNiDAYS); everything here is open.
- Unverifiable stat badges and audience-size claims.
- Ads adjacent to call-to-action buttons (AdSense placement policy).
